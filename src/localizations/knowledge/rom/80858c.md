The main loop's "wait for the frame": `$0100` is incremented by the NMI handler
(`80:823A`), so the loop ends on the next vertical blank. Cycles spent here are idle time, not
work: any cycle statistic (SA-1 candidates, slowdown hunts) must leave this routine out.
The 85 % figure is for the title/intro screens only; busy scenes (fights, big rooms) are not
measured yet.
