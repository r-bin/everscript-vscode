Doubly linked list over the 128 dynamic OBJ tile slots; a slot number is the OBJ tile number
(VRAM word `$6000 + slot*16`). `next[i]` is here, `prev[i]` at `$7E96A5`. Two sentinels:

| sentinel | list | role |
|---|---|---|
| `$80` | cache (LRU) | `next[$80]` is the most recently used, `prev[$80]` (`$7E9725`) the slot the allocator takes next |
| `$81` | used this frame | a cache hit unlinks its slot and appends it here; after the NMI `$80:8F85` splices this list onto the front of the `$80` list and empties it |

Tiles `$80` and above (the HUD portrait at `$80/$81/$90/$91`) are never slots, so a broken
link that leads to sentinel `$81` hands out OBJ tile `$81` and overwrites the portrait.
