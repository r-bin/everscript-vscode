Damage with a hit reaction. Reads and clears the one-shot `$23D5`: when it was set, the instruction runs as `0xBB` instead (same operands). Otherwise: entity and amount; skips an entity with 0 HP or `+$10` bit `$1000`; when the one-shot `$23D7` is set (and cleared) `$8F:C06D` transforms the amount; `$8F:C121` queues it as pending damage (`+$76`, `+$12` |= `$0038`).

## Why it exists

Traps, scripted hits.

## Notes

Previously believed: a set `$23D5` skips the damage.
