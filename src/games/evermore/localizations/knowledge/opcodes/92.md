Entity, amount. Skips dead or `$1000`-protected entities; `$23D7` (one-shot) transforms the amount (`$8F:C06D`); queues the damage with hit reaction (`$8F:C121`). When `$23D5` (one-shot) is set, it runs as `0xBB` instead.

## Why it exists

Traps, scripted hits.

## Notes

Previously believed: a set `$23D5` skips the damage.
