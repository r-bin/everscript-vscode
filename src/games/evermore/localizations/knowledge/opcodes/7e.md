Four expressions: divisor, source currency, multiplier, destination currency (offsets 0, 3, 6, 9). The source amount times the multiplier is divided by the divisor (SNES multiply/divide registers `$4202`-`$4216`, byte by byte); the quotient is added to the destination and the source keeps the remainder. Example: `currency_convert(2, 9, 1, 0)` turns credits into talons at 2:1.

## Why it exists

Money changers between the four eras.
