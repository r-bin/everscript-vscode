Unless a fade is already being set up (joypad edge word `$0105`), syncs the screen (`$80:85D8`); centres and clamps the camera (`$90:94AB`), sets brightness 0 (`$0106`, the INIDISP shadow) and asks for a stepped fade to 15 (`$0B83` = `$800F`), which the frame loop `$80:DE95` carries out.

## Why it exists

Room entries and cutscene ends.
