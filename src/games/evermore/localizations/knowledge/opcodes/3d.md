Entity expression and a word: the word (a trigger id: offset into the script pointer table at `$92:8000`) goes to the entity's event script `+$66`, and its event mask `+$68` becomes `$0040`: talk (B button within `$20` px, checked by `$8F:AC0E`). The 4-byte short form of `0x3F` for NPCs.

## Why it exists

Every talkable NPC: a 4-byte form of `0x3F` for the commonest case.
