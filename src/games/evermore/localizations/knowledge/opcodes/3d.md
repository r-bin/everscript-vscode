Entity expression; the word (a trigger id: offset into the pointer table at `$92:8000`) goes to entity `+$66`, the event mask `+$68` = `$0040` (talk / B button).

## Why it exists

Every talkable NPC: a 4-byte form of `0x3F` for the commonest case.
