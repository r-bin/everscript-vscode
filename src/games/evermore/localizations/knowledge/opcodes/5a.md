`$8C:C5E2`: locks the player (`$8C:C543`), clears the subtitle window and queue (`$8C:C4F6`), then runs field frames (`$8E:E41E`) while `$0B79` bit `$40` says text is still moving. Does not yield.

## Why it exists

Ends a block of subtitles.
