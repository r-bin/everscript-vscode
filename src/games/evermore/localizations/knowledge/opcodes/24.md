The handler table sends this byte to `$8C:E8B6`, one of the eight `STP` bytes at `$8C:E8B5`-`$E8BC`: the CPU stops and the game freezes.

## Why it exists

A hole in the opcode numbering. Seven of these bytes (`0x06`, `0x24`, `0x25`, `0x28`, `0x3E`, `0x40`, `0x41`) each point at their own STP byte, the others share `$8C:E8BC`: it looks like removed handlers whose labels were kept and pointed at a trap (guess).

## Notes

Cannot work. Not used by the original scripts; the decoder reports it as an error at its address.
