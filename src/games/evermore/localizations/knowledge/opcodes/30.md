Plays a sound effect. The byte is an offset into the word table `$8C:8362`; a negative entry is silent, any other is the driver's effect number, played by `$8C:82DC`. First the handler passes `$243B` to the sound driver (command `$30`) and clears it.

## Why it exists

Sound effects.

## Notes

Shares its handler with `0x31` and `0x32`.
