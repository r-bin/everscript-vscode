Finds the end of the shop list (`$0B95`, negative byte = end), then item and price expressions: the item's range sets `$0E6C` bit 2 (< 8), 4 (< `$30`) or 8; the byte from `$8C:E311` for the item goes into the list followed by the `$FF` end marker; the price goes to `$0BC5` + 2 * index.

## Why it exists

Shop lists.
