Six expressions: `$0E70` (scene), bytes `$0E74`-`$0E77`, a byte to `$0E79`. Runs the Mode 7 engine at `$80:BC00` (BGMODE 7, the flights) and restores the field.

## Why it exists

Flights (windwalker, rocket) and other Mode 7 scenes.
