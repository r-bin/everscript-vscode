Byte id of a global script: pointer = `$92:8000 + word[$92:8000] + word[$92:8008] + id * 3` (`word[$92:8008]` = `$1677` in the US ROM), then called as `0x29`. The entry reads at `$8C:D3B5`/`$D3BB` (`lda.l $928000,x`) are the ones the table hack rebases (`tools/table-hack`).

## Why it exists

Shared routines in 2 bytes.
