No operands. Locks the player (`$8C:C543`) and sets up the default window `$034D` at x 2, y 2, 28 x 10 with the player's style `$0B15`, then builds it like `0x44`.

## Why it exists

A short form of `0x44` for the standard box.

## Notes

Shares its handler with `0x49`-`0x4B`; none is used by the original scripts.
