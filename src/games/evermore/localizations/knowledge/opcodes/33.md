Changes the music. The byte is an offset into the word table `$8C:8442`; a negative entry changes nothing. With interrupts off (`$4200`, restored from `$0E6B`) it sends driver commands `$1A`, `$2A` with `$A0` (tempo, see `0x87`) and `$2C`, then starts the song (`$8C:828F`).

## Why it exists

Background music.
