The handler holds NMI palette uploads via `$7E:0F3C` and evaluates seven expressions: red, green and blue offsets, a mix weight, a colour, the first colour and a count. For each of `count` colours from `first` it takes the room's colour (the shadow at `$7E:6187`), mixes in `colour` by `mix`/256 (`$90:D34C`: 0 keeps the room's colour, 256 and up takes `colour`, between blends each channel through the hardware multiplier), adds the three offsets to the channels with clamping to 0-31, and writes the result to `$7E:65F1`; then it uploads that range through `$80:85FA`, releases the hold and returns with carry set.

The compiler-facing Everscript command is `color_filter()`; the parameters' names are in `script-vm/parameters.json` (2026-11-21, read from `$8C:E4B1`-`$8C:E5A0` and `$90:D34C`).
