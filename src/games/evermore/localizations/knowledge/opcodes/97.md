Seven expressions: palette range and per-channel offsets. Holds NMI palette uploads (`$0F3C` + 1), recomputes the colours from `$7E:6187` into `$7E:65F1` (`$90:D34C`, each channel clamped to 0-31), uploads them (`$80:85FA`) and releases the hold.

## Why it exists

Night, sepia, flashes.
