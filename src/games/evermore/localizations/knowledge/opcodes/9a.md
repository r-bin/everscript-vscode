Expression 0 or 2 indexes the 2-word table `$8C:E6E6` (`$0000`, `$0C82`); `$8C:9DAF` loads that font header from bank `$C4` (glyph sizes to `$0B3D`/`$0B43`/`$0B3F`, DP `$92`).

## Why it exists

The outro uses a different font.

## Notes

Values above 2 read past the 2-entry table: garbage.
