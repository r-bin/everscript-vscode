The handler table ends at `0xC2`, and the dispatcher has no range check. For this byte it reads the word at `$8C:EAA5`, which is code of the expression evaluator. That word sends the CPU to `$8C:6402`: hardware registers / open bus. That is not a handler.

## Why it exists

It does not: the opcode space ends at `0xC2`.

## Notes

Cannot work: running it crashes or corrupts the game. Not used by the original scripts; the decoder reports it as an error.
