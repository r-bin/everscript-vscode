Entity, x and y expressions (pixels) into DP `$46`/`$48`. An entity with `+$10` bit `$0008` is not moved. Otherwise `$8F:C2D1` moves it at once; when the one-shot layer override `$23DB` is set (not negative), it becomes the layer (`$44`), is reset to `$FFFF`, and `$8F:C2E4` places the entity on that layer.

## Why it exists

Instant repositioning of any actor.

## Notes

Walking instead of jumping: `0x9D`.
