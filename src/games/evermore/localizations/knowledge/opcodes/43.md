Entity, x, y expressions (pixels). Unless the entity has status bit `$0008`, it is moved at once: `$8F:C2D1`, or `$8F:C2E4` on the layer in `$23DB` when that one-shot override is set (it is reset to `$FFFF`).

## Why it exists

Instant repositioning of any actor.

## Notes

Walking instead of jumping: `0x9D`.
