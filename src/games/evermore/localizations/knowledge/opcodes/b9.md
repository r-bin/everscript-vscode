Entity, dx, dy expressions (pixels). Zero offsets, entity status bit `$0008`, or a failed probe (`$8F:AF48`) leave it where it is; otherwise `$8F:C2E4` moves it. `$23E3` = 1 when moved or nothing to do, 0 when blocked.

## Why it exists

Pushing, knockback.
