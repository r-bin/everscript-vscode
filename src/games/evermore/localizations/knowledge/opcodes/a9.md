Entity and an even selector, dispatched through `$8C:E6F8`. `$00`-`$0E`: the facing `+$22` = the selector. `$10`/`$12` clear/set `+$10` bit `$4000` (set also clears pending damage `+$76` and `+$12` bits `$0478`), `$14`/`$16` set/clear `+$10` bit `$0002` (set also clears pending damage), `$18`/`$1A` clear/set `+$12` `$1000`, `$1C`/`$1E` set/clear `+$12` `$4000`, `$20`/`$22` set/clear `+$10` `$0400`, `$24`/`$26` set/clear `+$10` `$0080`, `$28`/`$2A` set/clear `+$12` `$C000`, `$2C`/`$2E` set/clear `+$14` `$0020`, `$30`/`$32` set/clear `+$14` `$0040`, `$34`/`$36` set/clear `+$14` `$0800`. Everscript `ATTRIBUTE_BITS` names some: `$12` temporary invincibility, `$14` invincible, `$1A` root, `$20` no clip, `$28` invisible.

## Why it exists

One opcode for many on/off switches.

## Notes

Selectors `$38` and up jump into code bytes: garbage.
