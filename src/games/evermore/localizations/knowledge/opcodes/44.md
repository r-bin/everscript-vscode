Five bytes: window index (record `$034D + n * $2C`, `$8C:D967`), x, y, width, height. Locks the player (`$8C:C543`) and builds the window frame (`$8C:AC8A`). The frame style is the player's window setting `$0B15` (at `+$26`).

## Why it exists

Text boxes other than the default one.

## Notes

Shares its handler with `0x45`-`0x47`; `0x47` differs.
