Window index byte (record `$034D + n * $2C`), x, y, width, height bytes; the frame style comes from `$0B15` (the player's window setting).

## Why it exists

Text boxes other than the default one.

## Notes

Shares its handler with `0x45`-`0x47`; `0x47` differs.
