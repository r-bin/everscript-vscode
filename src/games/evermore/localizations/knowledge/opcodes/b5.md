Eight expressions: kind (index into `$90:D986`), source entity, x, y, z (DP `$46`/`$48`/`$4A`), target x, target y, last parameter; `$90:DCC0` fills the projectile (`+$20`, `+$22`, `+$24`, sometimes `+$28`) and starts its routine.

## Why it exists

Bombs, thrown objects, boss shots.
