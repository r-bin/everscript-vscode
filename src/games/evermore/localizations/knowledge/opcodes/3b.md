Sleeps for n frames: wakes at the frame counter `$0B19` + n - 1 (32-bit, slot `+$05`/`+$07`), thread state 4. n = 0 continues without yielding.

## Why it exists

Timed pauses with a computed length.

## Notes

Shares its handler with `0x39`; the constant forms are `0xA7`/`0xA8`.
