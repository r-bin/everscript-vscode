Entity expression, animation word, mode expression. A non-player entity with 0 HP is skipped when `+$10` bit `$0008` is set or `$23D9` is clear; the skip path clears `$23D9`. Otherwise the handler sets `+$2E` = `$0400`, changes `+$10` to `(old | $0040) & $DFFE`, and clears `+$0300` at `+$12`. The mode picks one of six handlers in the table at `$8C:E100`, then the animation starts through `$90:8124`: 0 nothing more (ONCE), 2 sets `+$12` bit `$0200` (ONCE_FREEZE), 4 sets `+$12` bit `$0100` (LOOP: `end_check` restarts the script), 6 sets `+$10` bit `$2000` (FREEZE), 8 clears the words at `+$01` and `+$03` (no name), 10 sets `+$10` bit `$0001` (PAUSE: hidden and paused). Word bit 15 clear: the animation id comes from `$C4:3C92 + word`; set: from `$8E:0032 + entity[+$60] + (word & $7FFF)`. See [the control-flow reconstruction](../../../../../../docs/disassembly/script-vm/animate.md).

## Why it exists

Every gesture in cutscenes.

## Notes

Odd modes read misaligned words from the dispatch table; values above 10 read beyond its six entries. Their behavior is not reconstructed.
