Entity, an animation word, a mode expression. A non-player entity with 0 HP is skipped unless `+$10` bit `$0008` is clear and the one-shot `$23D9` is set. Sets `+$2E` = `$0400`, `+$10` |= `$40` (and clears `$2001`), `+$12` &= ~`$0300`, then the mode (even) picks a routine at `$8C:E100`: 0 nothing more, 2 `+$12` |= `$0200`, 4 `+$12` |= `$0100`, 6 `+$10` |= `$2000`, 8 clears `+$01`/`+$03`, 10 `+$10` |= 1 (Everscript `ANIMATION_MODE`: once, once-freeze, loop, freeze, pause). Word bit 15 clear: the animation id comes from `$C4:3C92 + word`; set: from the entity's list `$8E:0032 + +$60 + (word & $7FFF)`. `$90:8124` starts it.

## Why it exists

Every gesture in cutscenes.

## Notes

An odd or larger mode jumps through bytes after the table: garbage.
