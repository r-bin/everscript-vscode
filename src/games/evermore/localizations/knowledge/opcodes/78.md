Entity, animation word, mode expression. Word bit 15 clear: global animation (`$C4:3C92`); set: the entity's own list (`$8E:0032 + +$60`); started by `$90:8124`. Mode (even, 0-10) picks a routine at `$8C:E100`: 0 once, 2 `+$12` |= `$200`, 4 `+$12` |= `$100`, 6 `+$10` |= `$2000`, 8 clears `+$01`/`+$03`, 10 `+$10` |= 1. Dead enemies are skipped unless `$23D9` (one-shot) is set.

## Why it exists

Every gesture in cutscenes.

## Notes

An odd or larger mode jumps through bytes after the table: garbage.
