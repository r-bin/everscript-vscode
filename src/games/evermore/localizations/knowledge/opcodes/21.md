Stops the field (`$80:85D8`), every script thread (`$8C:CEE9`) and the entities (`$8F:B38E`), places the party as `0x20`, then starts the room's enter script again (`$8C:CE99`). Ends the script.

## Why it exists

A warp inside the current map that re-runs the enter script, like `0x22` without loading another map.

## Notes

Not used by the original scripts.
