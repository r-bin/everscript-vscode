Unconditional jump: target = the address after the 2-byte operand + the operand as a signed word. Script code lives in the upper halves of banks (32 KB windows): crossing `$FFFF`/`$8000` moves to the next or previous bank (`$84` +/- 1). The taken side of every branch (`0x08`-`0x0B`, `0x8E`, `0x8F`) jumps into this handler too.

## Why it exists

Loops and `else` branches; also the taken side of every conditional (`0x08`-`0x0B`, `0x8E`, `0x8F` jump into this handler).

## Notes

Compiler output for `goto` and the end of an `if` body that has an `else`.
