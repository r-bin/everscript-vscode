Music volume step `$9B` = `$10`, then `$8C:8619`: adds the step to the music volume `$9A` and sends it with driver command `$28` (as `0x86`), until it saturates at `$FF`.

## Why it exists

Bring the music back after a quiet scene.

## Notes

Previously believed: a screen fade. It is the music volume.
