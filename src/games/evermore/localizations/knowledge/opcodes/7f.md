Word index into the 2-entry pointer table `$8C:E1FD` (0: `$7E:2210`, 3: `$7E:2234`), then the name entry screen (`$8D:8000`, `$8D:82CB`) edits that buffer and the field comes back. For `$7E:2234` it first copies a default name from `$C4:60AC` (`$80:B149`).

## Why it exists

Naming the Boy and the Dog.

## Notes

Index 3 (the Dog) is the only use. Other indexes read past the 2-entry table: garbage.
