Word index (0 or 3) picks the name buffer from `$8C:E1FD` and runs the name screen (`$8D:8000`, `$8D:82CB`), then restores the field.

## Why it exists

Naming the Boy and the Dog.

## Notes

Index 3 (the Dog) is the only use. Other indexes read past the 2-entry table: garbage.
