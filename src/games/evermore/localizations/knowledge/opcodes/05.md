Short backward jump: target = opcode address - (256 - byte). The byte is OR-ed with `$FF00`, so it can only go back, at most 256 bytes; then as `0x04`.

## Why it exists

A 2-byte form for tight `while` loops (wait until a flag changes), saving a byte against `0x04`.

## Notes

Cannot jump forward; a byte of 0 jumps 256 bytes back.
