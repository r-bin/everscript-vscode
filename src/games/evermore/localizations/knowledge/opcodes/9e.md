Caster entity, spell, level, then a 0-terminated list of target entities (an entry equal to `$3BC1` is skipped). The spell's routine comes from `$C4:5F97` / `$C4:5FC5` (`$91:973E`, `$91:AD17`); spells 2 and `$16` take one slot per target (`$91:9777`). Without a free slot it reads three more expressions and the list from where it stands: the same end only for lists of three or more.

## Why it exists

Scripted spells.

## Notes

Without a free slot it misreads the operands (handler entry).
