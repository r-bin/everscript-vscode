One byte of bits: `$08` the thread's bound entity (`slot + $0D`), `$01` the Boy (`$4E89`), `$02` the Dog (`$4F37`); each selected one is frozen through `$8F:CD78` (status bit `$0020`). `0xBC`, `0xBE`, `0xC0` enter at `$8C:DB31` with masks 1, 2, 3.

## Why it exists

One instruction for any combination of actors.

## Notes

Original scripts only use mask `$08`.
