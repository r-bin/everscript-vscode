Same handler as `0x44`, which compares its own opcode: for `0x47` the style is the fixed `$1924`, `+$2A` = 1 and the text is centred (`+$12` |= `$10`).

## Why it exists

Captions and narration without a frame.

## Notes

Not a pure alias: the only opcode whose handler reads the opcode byte back.
