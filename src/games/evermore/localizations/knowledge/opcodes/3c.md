Word = ENEMY value * 2 (character record from `$8E:B55A`), word = state (bit 15: a spawner, `$8F:DE98`), then x and y tiles. Otherwise spawns now (`$8F:B094`) and stores the entity at `$0341` (expression `entity_0341()`).

## Why it exists

Enter scripts place every actor this way.

## Notes

Short form `0xBA`, spawner form `0xC2`, pixel form `0xA2`.
