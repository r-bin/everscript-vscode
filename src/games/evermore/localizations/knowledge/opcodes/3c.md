Word = ENEMY value * 2 (character record from `$8E:B55A`), word = state, then x and y tile bytes (* 8 to DP `$46`/`$48`). The common tail `$8C:DD19` checks the spot (`$8F:A933`); state bit 15 makes a spawner record (`$8F:DE98`), otherwise the entity spawns now (`$8F:B094`) and is stored at `$0341` (expression `last_entity()`).

## Why it exists

Enter scripts place every actor this way.

## Notes

Short form `0xBA`, spawner form `0xC2`, pixel form `0xA2`.
