Expression = currency offset (0 talons, 3 jewels, 6 gold coins, 9 credits: 3-byte amounts at `$0AC6`), then a 24-bit constant (word + byte). Jumps (`$8C:D434`) when the amount is at least the constant.

## Why it exists

Shops and toll NPCs: "do you have enough money".

## Notes

The four currencies are 3 bytes each from `$0AC6` (Everscript `CURRENCY`).
