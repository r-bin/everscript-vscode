Expression = an entity, stored in `$0B73`; then `$8C:C627` with X = 1 opens the left speaker slot: it waits while the slot is closing (`$0B79` bit 8), then sets bit 4 (opening) with slide timer `$0B7B` = `$E0`. Each frame `$8C:C6CB` draws that entity's figure (`+$71`/`+$73`, palette `+$0C`) at the bottom left of the screen (`$80:9033`).

## Why it exists

Portraits of who is talking during subtitles (`subtext`).

## Notes

Not used by the original scripts.
