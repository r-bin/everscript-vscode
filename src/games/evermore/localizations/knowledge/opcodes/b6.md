Palette (8-15), first colour, last colour, speed expressions: loads that palette (`$90:CF3A`, variant `$23CB`) and starts rotating its colours first..last (`$90:D50F`); a negative speed runs the other way.

## Why it exists

Glowing and flashing tiles (lava, crystals, machines).

## Notes

Palettes below 8 are refused. Everscript calls it `tile_flashing`.
