Palette (8-15), first colour, last colour and speed expressions. `$8C:E899` loads the palette (from `$90:A7CB` + the variant offset `$23CB`, `$90:CF3A`), then `$90:D50F` starts rotating its colours first..last; a negative speed runs the other way.

## Why it exists

Glowing and flashing tiles (lava, crystals, machines).

## Notes

Palettes below 8 are refused. Everscript calls it `tile_flashing`.
