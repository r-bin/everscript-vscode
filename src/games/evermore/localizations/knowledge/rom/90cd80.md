Coordinates dynamic allocation of the 8 SNES sprite palette groups (palettes 8..15):
pins slots 0 and 1 for the Boy and Dog, searches dynamic slots 2..7 for deduplication
or free space via `$90:CDAF`, and executes slot 4 eviction if capacity is exceeded.
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/entities/palette-allocator.md`.
