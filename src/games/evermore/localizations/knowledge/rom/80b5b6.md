The reset code's last step. Because NMI is switched on just after vblank began, hardware fires the first
NMI immediately; an emulator that only delivers NMI at the start of vblank misses it and runs one NMI
behind from then on (SecretOfEvermoreRecomp docs/imperfections/late-nmi.md).
