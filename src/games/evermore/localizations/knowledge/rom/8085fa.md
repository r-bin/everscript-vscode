Uploads color palette data to PPU CGRAM using either direct hardware DMA Channel 1 or a 32-slot deferred transfer queue. When `$7E:0106` bit 7 is set, immediately acquires lock `$7E:0F3C`, programs destination `$2121` (`CGADD`), sets source `$28:$26` to `$2122` (`CGDATA`), triggers `$420B = 0x02`, and releases lock. Otherwise, packs a 7-byte descriptor into the deferred queue at `$7E:1292` and advances cursor `$7E:128E` (wrapping at 224 bytes) for commit during the next VBLANK interrupt.
