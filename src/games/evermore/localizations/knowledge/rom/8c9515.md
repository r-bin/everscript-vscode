Master level-based Max HP progression curve for the companion Dog across levels 1 through 99.
Contains 99 16-bit word values (198 bytes total, `$8C:9515`..`$8C:95DB`) indexing HP vitality before charms and gear additions:
- Level 1: 0 (effective base HP 36 with vitality base offset)
- Level 50: 468
- Level 99: 909

Read by `companion_stats_scaling_and_form_rebuild` (`$8F:857D`). When the Chocobo Egg passive charm (`$7E:2261 & 0x0040`) is acquired, the routine indexes the parallel amplified curve at `$8C:951F` instead.
