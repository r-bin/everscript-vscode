X selects a 3-byte reference (16-bit offset plus bank byte) in the table at $C4:0000. The routine stores it into the animation record Y twice-offset: the word goes to +$00 and to +$03, the bank to +$02, then +$0E is cleared and +$05 (probably a tick counter) is set to 1. The animation VM (rom/9080ce.md) then starts reading the script from that pointer.

The duplicate at +$03 is probably the restart pointer; not verified.
