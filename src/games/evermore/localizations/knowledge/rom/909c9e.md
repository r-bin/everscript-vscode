# room_lighting_ambient_tint_and_sine_pulsation_tick

Ticks sine wave lighting modulation on PPU window masks for glowing lava, lanterns, and underwater caustics.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$90:9C9E`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$90`
- **Size:** 125 bytes

## Functionality
Updates dynamic lighting effects such as pulsating torchlight, glowing magma caverns, or underwater ripple masks.
The routine loads base window mask boundaries into `$010E..$0114`, and invokes `$80:AD28` (`trig_sine_fixed_point_scale`) to compute cyclical modulation offsets.

It expands or contracts the lighting window mask diameter smoothly across frame ticks to simulate natural flame flicker and glowing aura pulsations.
