Master 64-bit multi-word pseudo-random number generator used throughout the Secret of Evermore engine for combat accuracy, damage variance, weather particle distribution, screen shake displacement, and AI decision making. Iteratively rotates and adds four 16-bit seed words across `$7E:0133`..`$7E:0139` with cross-word carry feedback, returning the primary updated random integer in Accumulator `$7E:0133`.
