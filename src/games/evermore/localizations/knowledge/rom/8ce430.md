First consumes `$23D5` by clearing it; when the old value was nonzero, it skips the remaining work. Otherwise it evaluates an entity and amount, skips the entity if its HP at `+$2A` is zero or flag `$1000` at `+$10` is set, and optionally transforms the amount when `$23D7` is nonzero (clearing that flag). It adds the resulting amount to pending damage at `+$76` and sets state bits `$0038` at `+$12`. Returns Carry Set.
