# room_lighting_torchlight_flicker_rng_jitter

Computes pseudo-random jitter offsets for room torchlight, fireplace, and lantern window masks.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$90:9ACF`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$90`
- **Size:** 108 bytes

## Functionality
Simulates realistic candle and torch flicker on circular lighting masks.
The routine centers the lighting window on protagonist coordinates `$7E:001A,X`, clamped to room bounds `$08F2`.

It invokes pseudo-random number generator `$80:859B` to inject random subtle radius perturbations into PPU window mask registers `$010E` and `$0112`, producing natural ambient firelight flicker across dungeon walls.

## Callers
- Ambient room lighting loops in Bank `$90`

## Callees
- `$80:859B` (`random_number_generator`)

## Variables and Memory
- `$59`, `$5B` (Camera world position coordinates)
- `$010E..$0114` (SNES PPU window mask boundary registers)
- `$08F2` (Room light mask radius threshold)
