Computes and applies the master sound volume step during room and scene transitions. Reads transition mode `$0E70` and frame counter `$0E72`, ramping volume between 0 and 15 (`$0F`) during fade-in and fade-out boundaries. Duplicates the 4-bit level into both nibbles to create an 8-bit master stereo volume byte in `Y`, and sends APU command `$28` to the SPC700 via `apu_command_handshake` (`$8C:81FD`).
