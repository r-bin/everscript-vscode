Main camera tracking and margin clamping routine. Determines the target focus point (either fixed
anchor coordinates `$7E:242B` / `$7E:242D` or the active player entity `$7E:0F42`), locks the VRAM
transfer queue (`$7E:0F3A++`), centers the $256 \times 224$ viewport over the target, clamps to the room's
bounding margins (`$7E:23E9`–`$7E:23EF`), stores the resulting view origin into DP `$BF`/`$C1`, and streams
background metatile edge updates before releasing the VRAM queue lock (`$7E:0F3A--`).
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/engine-core/camera-system.md`.
