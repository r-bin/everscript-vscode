Stepped PPU brightness fade-down loop. Reads current brightness/inidisp bits 2-3 from `$0106`. Saves into `$0B7F`, waits for NMI via `wait_for_nmi` (`$80:858C`), preserves forced blank bit 7, decrements brightness in steps of 4 (`SBC #$04`), and repeats until negative.
