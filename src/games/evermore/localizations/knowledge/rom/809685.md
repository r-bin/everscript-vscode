Initializes the 17-node circular doubly-linked active actor pool in WRAM bank `$7E`
(`$7E:50A7`–`$7E:512F`). Slot 0 (`$50A7`) acts as the sentinel head node (`status = $FFFE`).
Slots 1 through 16 (`$50AF`..`$5127`) are chained into the unallocated free list (`status = $FFFF`).
The upper pool boundary address `$512F` is stored into `$7E:1276`.
