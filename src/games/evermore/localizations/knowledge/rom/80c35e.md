# mode7_camera_altitude_zoom_perspective_calc

Calculates camera altitude scaling and ground perspective foreshortening factors in Mode 7 flight.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$80:C35E`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$80`
- **Size:** 132 bytes

## Functionality
Computes vertical camera scaling and ground perspective foreshortening during flight scenes.
The routine offsets camera focal altitude in `$0E72`, multiplies vertical scaling factors against pitch angle via hardware registers `$4202` and `$4216`, and invokes `$80:C308` to update the Mode 7 perspective transformation parameters.

## Callers
- `$80:C4A3` (`func_80C4A3`)
- `$80:CBD1` (`func_80CBD1`)

## Callees
- `$80:C308` (`func_80C308`)

## Variables and Memory
- `$0E70` (Flight operational mode status word)
- `$0E72` (Mode 7 camera altitude and focal distance)
- `$0E76` (Mode 7 camera vertical pitch scalar)
- `$4202`, `$4216` (Hardware multiplier and product registers)
