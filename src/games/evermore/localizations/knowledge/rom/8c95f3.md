Master level-based Physical Defense curve for the companion Dog across levels 1 through 99.
Contains 99 16-bit word values (198 bytes total, `$8C:95F3`..`$8C:96B8`) representing defense scaling before collar armor, gear, and charm bonuses.
Read by `companion_stats_scaling_and_form_rebuild` (`$8F:857D`). When the Armor Polish charm (`$7E:2262 & 0x0080`) is held, the routine indexes the parallel amplified curve at `$8C:95FD` instead.
