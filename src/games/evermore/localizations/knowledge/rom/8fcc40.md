Each tick, for every entity: if it is alive and its `mode` word `+$16` has neither bit `$10` nor `$08`,
its charge `+$2E` grows by the character record's `+$2E` (charge speed) up to `$400`. An attack's
`mode` sets bit `$10`, so nothing charges while swinging; a knockback's does not, so the meter fills
during knockback. The attack's last animation command `4E` empties it; an attack cut short by a hit
never reaches `4E` and keeps its charge.

The Boy or Dog that the player does not control charges past `$400`, by its speed − 3 a tick, up to
the cap its AI setting picks from `$8F:DDB3` (`$0B0B` Boy, `$0B0F` Dog).

Then, when `+$6C` bit 15 is set and `+$10 & $21` is clear, the behaviour routine runs
(`$8F:CD17`): every tick, not once per animation cycle. For the stationary plants (behaviour 8,
`$8F:D6FB`) that is the engage test `$8F:D69B`: full charge, then `rand & $FF` below the record's
`+$15` — 5 for the Wimpy Flower, about one tick in 51 — and the Boy, then the Dog, within the
record's `+$13` pixels on both axes. So a flower's wait between attacks is random.

The whole fight, its random rolls and the in-game check: `docs/entities/combat.md`.
