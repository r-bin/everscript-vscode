Called from the default melee hit `$8F:B75A` for every target a strike box touches. Before the roll
it writes the attacker into the target's `+$36` and 20 into `+$38`; the strike test `$8F:B61E` skips a
target whose `+$36` is the attacker. So an attack whose animation has several strike frames close
together (the Wimpy Flower's: ticks 6, 12 and 18) rolls once per target, hit or miss.

The chance comes from the table behind `$8F:BAAF`: the target's evade picks a row pointer, the
attacker's hit rate a word in it, `$7FFF` meaning certain.

The whole fight, its random rolls and the in-game check: `docs/entities/combat.md`.
