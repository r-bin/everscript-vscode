Configures the SNES hardware PPU horizontal/vertical timer interrupt (H/V-Timer IRQ). Disables interrupts, stores custom IRQ handler vector `X`/`A` into WRAM pointers `$012B` and `$012D`, programs target beam horizontal position from low byte of `Y` into `$4209` (`HTIMEL`) / `$010A`, programs target vertical scanline from high byte of `Y` into `$4207` (`VTIMEL`) / `$0108`, and enables timer IRQ, VBlank NMI, and joypad auto-read by writing `$B1` to `$4200` (`NMITIMEN`) and `$0E6B`.
