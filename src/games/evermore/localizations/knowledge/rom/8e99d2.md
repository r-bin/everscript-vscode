Iterates through character equipment slots (helmets/collars, vests, bracers, accessories):
1. Verifies equipped items against inventory bitmasks at `$C4:59A4` / `$C4:59C0`.
2. Accumulates physical defense into `$7E:07A4` and magic defense into `$7E:07A6`.
3. Propagates aggregated defense values to character stat blocks (`$7E:0A26` Boy, `$7E:0A70` Dog).
