Checks entity animation flag bit `$2000` in `+$3C,Y`. If set, clears direction offset `<$02>`, sets direction `<$04>` from `+$22,Y`, and dispatches movement step via `Entity_mover_step_direction_table_8F_AF18_8FAD51` (`$8F:AD51`).
