Master signed 16-bit trigonometric sine table of 512 entries (1024 bytes). Divides a complete
$360^\circ$ circle into 512 discrete angle steps. Cosine values are retrieved directly from the
same table by applying a quarter-turn phase shift of $+128$ steps (`(angle + 128) & 0x01FF`).
Used by the Mode 7 overworld flight engine (`$8E:B33D`), projectile ballistic homing vectors (`$8F:B094`),
and orbiting ring menu icon coordinates.
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/engine-core/mode7-flight.md`.
