Signed 16-bit sine values, 256 steps for a whole turn, from -255 to +256 (8.8 fixed point:
256 means 1.0). The table is stored 320 entries (640 bytes) long, a quarter turn more than a full
circle, so cosine is read from the same table 64 entries on (`$80:8923`) without wrapping.
Used by the Mode 7 overworld flight engine (`$8E:B33D`), projectile vectors (`$8F:B094`), the
orbiting projectiles such as the boomerang (`$90:DF44`, with `$80:8923` for x) and the ring menu.
The Data tab draws it (Tables › Sine helper, Boomerang orbit).
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/engine-core/mode7-flight.md`.

## Notes
- Previously believed: 512 entries (1024 bytes) with cosine at +128 entries. The `$01FF` mask
  applies to the byte offset (angle × 2), so the turn is 256 entries; the ROM data repeats after 256.
