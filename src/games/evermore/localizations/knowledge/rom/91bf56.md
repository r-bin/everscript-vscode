# projectile_homing_trajectory_and_jitter_tick

Adjusts projectile homing vectors towards active targets, injecting pseudo-random trajectory deviations.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$91:BF56`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$91`
- **Size:** 356 bytes

## Functionality
Updates guidance vectors for homing projectiles and guided spells (such as Crush or Acid Rain droplets).
The routine compares current projectile heading `$14E6` against target bearing `$14E8`, adjusting heading step `$14F4` towards convergence.

It decrements a jitter countdown timer in `$14FA`; when exhausted, it calls `$80:859B` (`random_number_generator`) to inject subtle pseudo-random flight perturbations, producing organic curving paths rather than rigid straight lines.
