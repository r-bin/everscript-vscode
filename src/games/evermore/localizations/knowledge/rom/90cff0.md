Reduces the brightness of 112 colors in palette staging buffer `$7E:61A9` by 50% for menu transitions. Operates on 15-bit BGR555 words via logical shift right (`LSR`) masked with `#$3DEF` to isolate color channels, writing darkened values back to `$7E:61A9` and uploading 222 bytes to CGRAM via `$80:85FA`.
