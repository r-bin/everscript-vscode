Loads a merchant's active stock stream into WRAM:
- Iterates stream bytes at `$0B95,y` until encountering terminator `0x80`.
- Looks up item prices and category attributes in master ROM table `$C4:5ABE,x`.
- Stores active catalog records into WRAM buffer `$0BA5,x`.
- Tracks total merchandise item count in `$0BA1` and clamps cursor `$0BA3`.
