Turns a signed byte vector (dx in DP `$12`, dy in DP `$13`) into one of four codes by its dominant
axis: vertical wins ties. Unlike its neighbours `$8F:A963`, `$8F:A97D` and `$8F:A999` it ends in
RTL. Not called during the 2:20 no-input intro; callers not yet known.
