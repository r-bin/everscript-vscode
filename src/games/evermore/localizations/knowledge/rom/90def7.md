Converts polar velocity angle `+$1A,Y` and position `(+$14,Y, +$16,Y)` into Cartesian coordinate offsets using trigonometric tables `$80:8923,X` and `$80:88A3,X`, storing resulting screen trajectory into `+$20,Y` and `+$22,Y`.
