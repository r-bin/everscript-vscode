Eight `STP` bytes. 27 opcodes have no handler and point here, so the CPU stops: `0x06` → `$E8B5`, `0x24` → `$E8B6`, `0x25` → `$E8B7`, `0x28` → `$E8B8`, `0x41` → `$E8B9`, `0x40` → `$E8BA`, `0x3E` → `$E8BB`, and `0x01`-`0x03`, `0x0F`, `0x13`, `0x1F`, `0x2F`, `0x34`-`0x37`, `0x4F`, `0x53`, `0x56`, `0x57`, `0x67`, `0x6B`, `0x72`, `0x8B`, `0xB8` → `$E8BC`. The halt address names the group. Opcodes `0xC3`-`0xFF` do not come here: they read past the table (`soe://rom/assets/scripts/opcodes/`).
