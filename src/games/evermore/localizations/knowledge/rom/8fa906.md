Extracts the 2-bit elevation plane (bits 5..4) from a metatile collision word into Direct
Page `$44`. Evaluates bit 13 (always-walkable / conveyor) and bit 6 (plane transparency / bridge
traversal): if crossing onto a bridge, sets bit 6 (`ORA #$0040`); if stepping off, clears bit 6
via `TRB $44` to latch the entity into the new ground plane.
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/maps/elevation-transfer.md`.
