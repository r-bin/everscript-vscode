Master level-based Hit Rate curve for the companion Dog across levels 1 through 99.
Contains 99 16-bit word values (198 bytes total, `$8C:90BF`..`$8C:9184`) representing accuracy scaling before gear and charm bonuses.
Read by `companion_stats_scaling_and_form_rebuild` (`$8F:857D`). When the Accuracy Charm (`$7E:2262 & 0x0001`) is held, the routine indexes the parallel amplified curve at `$8C:90C9` instead.
