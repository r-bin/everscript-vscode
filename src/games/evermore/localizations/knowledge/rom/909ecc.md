Evaluates bit 14 (`0x4000`) of the 16-bit room header flags word at `$7E:0F84` (header bytes 9–10).
When bit 14 is set on Mode 7 overworld maps, camera vertical view origin (`$C1`) is biased downwards relative to vehicle flight altitude (`$7E:0617 - 96`) to maintain correct horizon line perspective during flight.
