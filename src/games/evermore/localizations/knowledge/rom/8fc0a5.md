Adds A to the HP word (+$2A) of the entity in Y and caps the result at that entity type's maximum, read from the per-type record at $8E:0000 + [Y+$60], field +$0F. For party entities (address $4E89 and above) the HUD text is refreshed with rom/8f9144.md. Afterwards +$76 is cleared and bits $0478 of the flag word +$12 are cleared.

The +$76 and +$12 clearing is observed, but its purpose is not known.

Previously believed: some of the guess entries from the v0.387.7-12 batches treat +$2A as an action/animation state; it is the HP word.
