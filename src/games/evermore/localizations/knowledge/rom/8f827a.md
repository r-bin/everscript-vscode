# combat_party_exp_award_and_level_up_check

Awards defeated enemy experience points to the party, checks level thresholds, and triggers level up.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$8F:827A`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$8F`
- **Size:** 131 bytes

## Functionality
Handles experience point distribution and level-up advancement upon defeating enemies.
The routine loads the defeated monster\'s EXP reward from ROM table `$8E:0023,X` and adds it to the player\'s 32-bit accumulated EXP in `$0A49..$0A4B`.

It checks level cap bounds (level 99 / `#$63`) and compares total EXP against the next level requirement in `$4F1F`. When a threshold is met, it invokes `$8F:8310` (`boy_level_up_and_stat_rebuild`) and `$8F:84F5` (`dog_level_up_and_form_rebuild`) to increment levels and recompute combat attributes.

## Callers
- `$90:84A7` (`enemy_death_and_loot_drop_processor`)

## Callees
- `$8F:8310` (`func_8F8310`)
- `$8F:84F5` (`func_8F84F5`)

## Variables and Memory
- `$0A49`, `$0A4B` (Player 32-bit accumulated experience points)
- `$0A50` (Current party member level number)
- `$4F1F` (32-bit experience points required for next level)
- `$8E:0023` (ROM monster experience reward table)
