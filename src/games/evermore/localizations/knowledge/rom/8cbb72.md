Executes a synchronous full-screen mosaic pixelation swell transition. Writes to SNES PPU register `$2106` (`MOSAIC`) with BG enable bits set, incrementally increasing mosaic block size from 1 to 5 (`$1F`..`$5F`, step `$10`) with VBlank pauses via `$8C:B63B`, then smoothly decrements back down to 0, clearing `$2106` upon completion.
