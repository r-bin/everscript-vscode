# combat_player_charge_attack_gauge_tick

Advances player weapon charge timers, transitioning across charge levels and triggering weapon ready flashes.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$8F:946B`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$8F`
- **Size:** 202 bytes

## Functionality
Manages the player\'s weapon charge accumulation while holding the attack button.
The routine verifies weapon charge readiness, advancing charge timer variables according to weapon level (Level 1, 2, or 3).

When a threshold is reached, it triggers palette flash animations and cues audio via `$8F:93A0`, indicating that an enhanced charge attack can be unleashed upon releasing the attack button.
