Queues an amount as pending damage for an entity. It evaluates an entity expression and a damage expression, and only queues the amount if the entity has nonzero current HP and bit `$1000` is clear in the entity flags at `+$10`. The helper adds the amount to the entity's pending-damage field at `+$76` and sets bits `$0018` in its state field at `+$12`; later damage processing applies the queued amount to HP and handles the damage reaction and number display.

Operands: `E E` (entity, amount; tokens: docs/game-research/script-vm/README.md). Flow: continues in the same turn.

- Calls: `$8F:C16E`

- Opcode table entry: `$8C:EA33` (0xbb)
