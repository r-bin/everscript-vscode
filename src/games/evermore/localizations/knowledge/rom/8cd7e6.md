Opcodes `0x12` and `0x16` share this handler. It computes a destination at the active script slot plus `$0F` plus the one-byte operand, evaluates an expression, stores its low byte through data bank `$7E`, and returns carry set for same-turn continuation. The expression evaluator owns any additional script-pointer advancement.
