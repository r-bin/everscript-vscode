Synchronizes the 3D world coordinates and elevation layer plane of an attached child entity to its host parent:
1. Resolves child pointer from the active entity pool at `$7E:50A7,X`.
2. Locks the child's position to the parent entity's world coordinates (`parent->x`, `parent->y`, `parent->z`).
3. Matches the collision layer plane (`+$18`) to ensure correct multi-layer ramp and elevation sorting.
4. Invokes `entity_set_position_with_z` (`$8F:C2E4`) to refresh tile collision and screen projection.

Supports:
- Ground shadow attachments (`entity->shadow_entity_id` at `+$6A`)
- Carried/held weapon attachments (`entity->held_entity_id` at `+$6C`)
- Auxiliary defensive bubble / barrier aura attachments (`entity->aux_entity_id` at `+$98`)
