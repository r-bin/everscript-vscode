Shaft drop routine for diagonal right-curving drops in Bugmuck (Room 22).

Commands the character to walk to `(TARGET_WAYPOINT_X, TARGET_WAYPOINT_Y)`, triggers the free-fall loop animation, and teleports diagonally down and to the right by shifting `X += (arg[8] << 1)` each frame until reaching the destination.
