The `ENEMY` id a script spawns is an index into this table, not a record number. Each word is the
16-bit address of a character record in the record bank (`$8EB678 + 74·n` in vanilla).

Entries 0 and 1 are `$0A26` and `$0A70`: the Boy's and the Dog's records live in WRAM. Readers
reach them through the WRAM mirror at the bottom of the record bank, so that bank must stay a
`$80–$BF` bank. One entry is 0, and every other entry is a ROM record.

`0xBA` and `0xC2` take a one-byte id, so 256 ids at most.
