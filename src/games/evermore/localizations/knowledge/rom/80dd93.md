Simultaneously fades screen brightness and audio volume down to zero frame-by-frame. Waits for each VBlank (`$80:858C`), decrements PPU brightness shadow `$0106`, scales brightness by 16 (`ASL x 4`) into stereo volume byte `Y`, and sends APU command `$28` via `$8C:81FD` until complete silence and blackness are reached.
