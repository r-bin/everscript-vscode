Records each new chord of held buttons and matches the newest chords against its table. In the US
game the only entry is Select alone, which switches control between the boy and the dog
(`$8C:F17C`). Runs only while the field tick runs (not during the boot logos, $0F42 = 0), and on
the title screen and in cutscenes too.

## Cycles (snes9x2005 and hardware, FastROM)
From `$8C:F134` (chord changed) to the exit `$8C:F16C`: 78 fetch/internal cycles and 21 WRAM
cycles when the previous chord was not empty, 72/20 after a release; to `$8C:F156` (Select
matched) 64/20 and 58/19. A hook replacing this path has to spend exactly that, or replays of
vanilla recordings drift (`hacks/hotkeys/hotkey_hack.asm` in the everscript repo does).

## Write order
`$3BBE` (when the slot is kept), `$3ABE,x`, DP `$28`-`$29` (= `$008C`), DP `$26`-`$27`
(= `$F21A`), then the table index 0 on the stack.
