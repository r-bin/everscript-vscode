Reads a 16-bit string key and passes it to `$8C:CD33` for resolution and decompression. It then runs a fixed field-transition sequence, clears `$0106`, sets `$0B83` to `$800F`, and clears `$143A` before returning Carry Set. The purposes of the other internal transition calls are not fully established from this handler alone.
