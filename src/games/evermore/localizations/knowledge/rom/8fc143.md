Applies strike damage or status effect buildup to an entity (`Y`). Verifies that entity status word `+$10,Y` does not have invulnerability flag `$4000` set and `+$14,Y` does not have bits `$0109` set. Accumulates incoming amount `A` into pending damage accumulator `+$76,Y` and raises hit reaction / flinch status bits `$0418` on entity flag word `+$12,Y`.
