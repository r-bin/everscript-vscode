Updates and advances active colour cycles each frame during the main map engine loop (`$90:CBB3`). For each active cycle slot in `$1372..$1411` (up to 16 slots, 2 per palette), it decrements the step hold countdown timer at `$13F2,X`. When the timer expires, it rotates the palette color words across the configured span at `$1372,X` and queues a DMA update to SNES CGRAM (`$2122`).
