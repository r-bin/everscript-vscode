# entity_shadow_oam_sprite_render

Renders dynamic ground shadow sprites beneath airborne and jumping entities, scaling with Z elevation.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$90:DB9E`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$90`
- **Size:** 131 bytes

## Functionality
Draws ground shadow sprites beneath entities with non-zero Z elevation (such as jumping characters, airborne monsters, or floating spells).
The routine checks entity active flags at `$6387`, calculates ground-level coordinates by projecting entity world position down to the ground plane, and calls `$80:960A` to emit translucent shadow sprite cel entries into the OAM buffer.

## Callers
- `$8E:E41E` (`FUN_8ee41e`)

## Callees
- `$80:960A` (`FUN_80960a`)

## Variables and Memory
- `$6387` (Active jumping/flying entity record structure)
- `$1276` (OAM depth-sorted sprite queue pointer)
- `$16,X` (Entity Z elevation coordinate)
