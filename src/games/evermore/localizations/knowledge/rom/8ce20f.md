With no operands, restores the four color-math values saved at `$7E:235B-$235E` to PPU registers `$212C`, `$212D`, `$2131`, and `$2130`. It clears bit 0 of `$0B29`, then sets that bit when bit 7 of `$7E:22EA` is set. Returns Carry Set.
