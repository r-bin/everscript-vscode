Five bytes: window index (record `$034D + n * $2C`, `$8C:D967`), x, y, width, height. Locks the player (`$8C:C543`) and builds the window frame (`$8C:AC8A`). The handler compares its own opcode: `0x44`-`0x46` take the player's window style `$0B15` at `+$26`; `0x47` takes the fixed style `$1924`, `+$2A` = 1 and centres the text (`+$12` |= `$10`) — Everscript `MESSAGE_BOX_TYPE.TRANSPARENT_CENTERED`.
