Called immediately upon selecting an alchemy formula to cast:
- Validates that player owns required quantities for Ingredient 1 and Ingredient 2 using table `$C4:601F`.
- Deducts ingredients from `$7E:22FF`–`$7E:2314`.
- Increments formula usage stat counter at `$7E:2FDE + X`.
- Awards formula experience to `$7E:2F52 + X` based on current level (table `$C4:5B9C`), advancing level at 100 XP (cap Level 9:99).
