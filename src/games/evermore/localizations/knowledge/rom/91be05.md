Advances the ambient weather particle engine by one simulation frame. Ticks gravity and fall physics (`$91:BE42`), horizontal wind drift (`$91:BF56`), screen boundary wrapping (`$91:C0BA`), foreground collision/depth sorting (`$91:C11F`), and commits active particles to OAM (`$91:C15B`). Alternates particle double-buffer base `$14EE ^= $80` and synchronizes particle tracking coordinates from camera scroll registers `$010E` and `$0110`.
