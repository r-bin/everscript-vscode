Handles backspacing or clearing text glyphs during dialogue menu choice selection. Reads character at `[<$22],Y` and clears it in the buffer. For spaces (`$20`), subtracts standard indentation `+$04,X`; for characters, queries variable-width metric table `[<$92],Y`, adjusts cursor position, and calls `dialogue_glyph_decode_and_width_lookup` (`$8C:A928`).
