Part of the OAM builder: combines a sprite chunk's own attribute byte (`$DF`, from the
sprite data) with the character's attribute word from `$8FC773` (`$06`/`$07`). The mask
in `$07` decides which of the chunk's bits survive; the priority in `$06` is added. With
the usual mask `$CC` the chunk's priority bits (4–5) are dropped and the whole sprite gets
the character's priority; with `$FC` (a gate-nibble-4 tile) they are added, so the sprite
is split by its chunks' marks.
