Returns in Y the first free slot of the 8-slot animation-alchemy table (ram/7e3364.md, 64-byte stride), or Y = 0 when every slot is taken. A slot is free when its word at +$14 is zero. Callers claim the slot themselves; this routine does not write anything.

The sibling for projectile spells is rom/919777.md (118-byte stride). The +$14 field is not yet in the RAM entry.
