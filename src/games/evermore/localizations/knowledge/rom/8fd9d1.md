After an enemy has engaged, it attacks only when the target is lined up with it on one axis: within
the record's `+$13` along that axis, and within half the target's radius plus 5 across it. The
region is a plus sign, not a square. `+$13` is therefore both the engage distance
(`$8F:D6CE`, a square) and the attack reach.
