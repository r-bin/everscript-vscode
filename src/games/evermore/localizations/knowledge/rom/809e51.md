Handles particle ground impact, splash droplet keyframe sequencing, and top boundary recycling:
1. Checks if the active weather preset defines a ground splash animation base tile in `$7E:7311`.
2. For rain with splash enabled:
   - Initiates a 3-frame ground impact animation (`phase_timer = 3` in `$7E:2480,X`).
   - Cycles through splash ripple sprites without moving downward until animation completes.
3. For snow, embers, or completed splash frames:
   - Resets vertical position to top spawn edge `$7E:72DD`.
   - Randomizes horizontal spawn position within `[$7E:72D9, $7E:72DB]` using pseudo-random generator `$80:859B`.
