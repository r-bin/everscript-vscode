Shaft drop routine used across the Bugmuck pit chambers (Room 22).

Commands the target character to walk to the designated entry coordinates `(arg[4], arg[6])` (`TARGET_WAYPOINT_X`, `TARGET_WAYPOINT_Y`), initiates the free-fall loop animation (`FALL_1` for dog, `ACT1_FALL1` for boy), and smoothly drops the character vertically by 4 pixels per frame to the destination floor.
