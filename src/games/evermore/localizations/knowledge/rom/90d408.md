Configures a fog or weather particle emitter descriptor in WRAM. Populates an 8-entry coordinate array at `$7E:65D1` with X/Y pairs from direct page `$02` and `$04`, sets drift speed, particle density, and layer depth parameters in `$150E`, `$1510`, and `$1512`, and arms the particle emitter by setting bit 0 on state word `$150C`.
