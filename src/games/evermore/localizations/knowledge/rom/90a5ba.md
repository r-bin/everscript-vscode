Synchronously flushes all pending minimap radar tile blits to VRAM. Repeatedly invokes `hud_minimap_vram_blit` (`$90:A4A6`), sets the remaining chunk budget `$7E:116E` to 30 (`0x001E`), and halts CPU thread execution across vertical blanking intervals via `wait_for_frame` (`$80:858C`) until the Carry flag returns clear, ensuring all queued radar cell tile DMA transfers are committed before terrain mutation or script execution advances.
