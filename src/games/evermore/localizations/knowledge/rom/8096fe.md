Allocates an active VRAM tile slot for a 16x16 sprite macro-block (4 OBJ tiles = 128 bytes).
Unlinks the oldest slot from LRU queue `$7E:AB78`, moves it to MRU head `$7E:AB79`, updates
forward mapping `$7E:97A9` and reverse mapping `$7E:AA58`, resolves VRAM target word address from
`$80:9539,X`, queues the destination into DMA upload buffer `$7E:163C`, and initiates decompression
from master table `$EC:0000,X` via `$80:9741`.
