Validates player item, spell, or equipment selection in the circular ring menu system.
Resolves the selected icon's 8-byte item descriptor via pointer table `$8E:8000`, extracts its
sub-item identifier (e.g. formula index or inventory ID), checks whether the player can afford
the transaction against currency and item limits, and either executes the selection or sets the
rejection bit in `$7E:05A9` to trigger an error buzz sound.
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/ui/ring-menu.md`.
