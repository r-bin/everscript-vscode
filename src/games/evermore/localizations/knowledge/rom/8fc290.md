Initiates damage recoil and flinch behavior when an entity receives a valid strike:
1. Inverts the entity's facing direction 180 degrees (`entity->facing_direction ^= 0x0008`) if knockback velocity is non-zero.
2. Sets Bit 6 (`0x0040`: In Hit Recoil / Invulnerable) and clears Bit 0 in `entity->status_flags` (`+$10`).
3. Clears animation transit bits (`entity->anim_state &= ~0x0300`).
4. If entity is player or companion (`address >= $4E89`), asserts flinch flag (Bit 10: `0x0400`) in `character_flags` (`+$14`).
5. Reads the damage animation track ID from character descriptor `+0x40` (`$8E:0000 + entity->anim_table_ptr`) at `$8F:C2C4`.
6. Zeroes out the latch countdown timer (`+$78`).
7. Jumps via `JML $908124` to begin the hit reaction animation track in the animation VM.

## Addresses
- Facing Direction Field: `+$22`
- Status Flags Field: `+$10` (Bit 6 `0x0040` = hit recoil)
- Character Flags Field: `+$14` (Bit 10 `0x0400` = player flinch)
- Animation Descriptor Base: `$8E:0000`
- Animation Engine Entry: `$90:8124`
