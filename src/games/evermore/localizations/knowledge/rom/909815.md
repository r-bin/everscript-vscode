X and Y are world pixel coordinates. The routine converts them to a map cell (16-pixel tiles, word-sized cells), reads the cell word through the pointer kept in $56, and uses that word as an index into the table at $7F:0004, returning the property word found there in A. The row pitch factor is $08EE (ram/7e08f8.md).

The bank of the pointer in $56/$58 and the meaning of the returned word are not verified; presumably it is the collision/terrain word used by entity movement.
