Ticks the master music volume fade progression:
- Tests Direct Page `$9B` (signed fade target).
- Steps current level by rate `$9A` toward ceiling or floor.
- Once fade completes (`$9B = 0`), issues APU IPC Command `0x0028` or `0x00A9` across hardware port
  `$2140` to commit final volume.
Full disassembly and clean C reconstruction: `docs/disassembly/audio/positional-audio.md`.
