Fast-path sprite drawer for animation records. If sprite definition pointer A is non-zero, sets `<$26>`, reads bank from `+$08,X` into `<$28>`, merges palette flags from `+$0C,X` into `<$06>`, and jumps to `direct_sprite_draw_oam` (`$80:9033`).
