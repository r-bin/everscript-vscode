Populates Low OAM attribute buffer for HUD window border sprites. Sets data bank to `$7E`. Fills 32 sprite attribute words at `$ACC1` with `$36`, next 32 words with `$38`, and sets high-table size bits to `$AAAA` (all large 16x16 sprites) across 8 words at `$ABDE + $20E`.
