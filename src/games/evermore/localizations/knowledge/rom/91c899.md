# collision_tilemap_spatial_raycast_and_barrier_test

Raycasts projectile and spell trajectories across room tilemap collision masks to detect terrain impacts.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$91:C899`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$91`
- **Size:** 677 bytes

## Functionality
Performs spatial barrier collision tests for moving projectiles, spell particles, and environmental effects.
The routine translates world coordinate deltas into 16x16 collision tile indices, comparing trajectory vector masks in `$158E` and `$158C`.

It tests room collision words for solid walls, impassable barriers, water, and elevation boundaries. If a collision is encountered, it computes the exact contact point, terminates projectile motion, and flags the entity for impact explosion or splash sequencing.
