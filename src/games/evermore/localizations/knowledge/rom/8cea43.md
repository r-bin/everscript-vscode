Evaluates the expression at the script pointer into a 32-bit value (DP `$02` low, `$04` high) and
returns the low word in A. Each byte's low 7 bits pick a sub-op from `$8C:F041`; bit 7 marks the
last byte. Called by about 70 opcode handlers. `$8C:EC8E` is the same plus storing the result
as the entity to act on (DP `$4C`, also in Y). Format: docs/scripts/vm.md.
