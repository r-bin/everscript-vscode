Initializes an allocated entity slot for new projectiles or NPCs:
- Increments active and total entity count registers `$1072` and `$1074`.
- Injects origin coordinates `$46` and `$48`.
- Cleanses all 4 status effect slots (`+$46`, `+$4C`, `+$52`, `+$58`) by writing `$FFFF` to prevent stale buff/ailment corruption.
- Sets parent entity link `+$60` and default heading `+$22 = 0x0008` (Down).
