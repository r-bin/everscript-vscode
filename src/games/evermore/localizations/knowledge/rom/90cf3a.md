Every sprite palette the game loads goes through this pair. Character record `+0x09` and `+0x0B`,
the weapons' `+0x04` and the dog forms' `+6` are all 16-bit addresses in bank `$90`. Every vanilla
palette sits in the upper half (≥ `$8000`). Bank `$90` has no free space, so more palettes need a
hook here or room freed elsewhere in the bank.

The Boy's and Dog's low-HP flash (`$8F:9583`, `$8F:95EB`, `$8F:966E`, `$8F:96D6`) and `$90:8A2F`
also set up a bank-`$90` pointer. They read the party's own palettes.
