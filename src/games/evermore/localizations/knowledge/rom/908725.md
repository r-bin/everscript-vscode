Low confidence — Spawns a projectile entity at relative muzzle/launch offsets from the launching entity.

> Inferred from a partial read of the routine; names, purposes and variable roles below are hypotheses until the whole body is traced or tested.

Instantiates projectiles fired by enemies, bosses, or party members.
The routine reads relative launch offsets from weapon/spell descriptor table `[$5D]`.

It adds signed horizontal and vertical offsets to parent entity world coordinates (`$1A,X`, `$1C,X`), stores the calculated origin in `$46` and `$48`, and calls `$90:DCA4` (`Spawn_projectile_90DCA4`) to spawn the active projectile entity.

## Callers
- Entity weapon and projectile firing routines in Bank `$90`

## Callees
- `$90:DCA4` (`Spawn_projectile_90DCA4`)

## Variables and memory (hypotheses)
