# mode7_flight_heading_yaw_rotation_matrix_calc

Calculates yaw rotation matrix factors and bank angle tilt scalars during Mode 7 flight turns.

## Metadata
- **Address:** `$80:D934`
- **Type:** `routine`
- **Bank:** `$80`
- **Size:** 193 bytes

## Functionality
Calculates banking rotation angles and heading trigonometry for Mode 7 aircraft navigation.
The routine subtracts directional offsets from heading angle `$BB`, scales pitch altitude `$14B8`, and invokes trigonometric solver `$80:DA6B` to generate banking matrix factors that bank the camera during sharp turns.

## Callers
- `$80:C95B` (`FUN_80c95b`)

## Callees
- `$80:DA6B` (`func_80DA6B`)

## Variables and Memory
- `$BB` (Current aircraft heading orientation angle)
- `$BD` (Adjusted turn delta angle)
- `$14B8` (Aircraft altitude / pitch angle)
- `$02`, `$04` (Matrix banking transformation parameters)
