Commits 16-color palette records into the CGRAM shadow buffer:
- Unrolls sequential word stores across `$7E:7341` through `$7E:734F`.
- Increments source index by 16 bytes (`ADC #$0010`).
- Prepares palette data for high-speed DMA or unrolled transfer during VBLANK NMI (`$80:823A`).
