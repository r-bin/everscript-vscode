Mutual symmetric collision evaluation between two overlapping entities (X and Y).
Requires the charging entity to have collision bits `$0016 & 0xC000` set and charge stamina `$002E >= 0x0400` (1024). Validates target invulnerability masks (`$0016 & 0x0021`, `$0010 & 0x5022`, `$0014 & 0x070D`), current HP (`$002A > 0`), and damage source (`$0036 != attacker`). Dispatches contact attack action via `$8E:0030` and handler table `$8F:B6AE,x`, clears charge meter (`$002E = 0`), and repeats identically with entities swapped.
