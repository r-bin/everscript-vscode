Draws one multi-part sprite record at screen position `$02`/`$04` with attribute word `$06`/`$07`.
Performs coarse bounding box culling on descriptors with $\ge 3$ slices against the $256 \times 224$ viewport.
Dispatches visible slices to 8x8 and 16x16 geometry clippers, queries dynamic OBJ VRAM caches, and blits to OAM.
