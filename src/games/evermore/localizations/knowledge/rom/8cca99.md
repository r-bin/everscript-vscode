Decrements the outer iteration loop counter in DP `$04` during the first pass of the 0x93 tile decoder command.
The loop executes 32 (`0x20`) times, streaming 32 words of tile graphics to VRAM before branching to the pass transition at `$8C:CAE4`.

## Addresses
- Counter Variable: DP `$04`
- Transition Branch: `$8C:CAE4`
