Called during frame tick to simulate ambient environmental effects:
- Checks screen shake flag in direct page `$C3`.
- Decays shake intensity register `$7E:2435` and generates oscillating vertical camera offset into `$7E:2437`.
- Updates weather particle positions (rain droplets, snow flurries, embers) and wraps particles across screen bounds.
