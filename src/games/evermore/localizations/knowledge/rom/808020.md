Native mode, stack $1FFF, D=0; clears PPU/CPU registers; fills VRAM and both WRAM banks by fixed-source DMA; PPU shadow defaults ($0106=$8F); seeds the RNG $0133-$0139 from the PPU H/V counters; enables NMI + auto-joypad ($4200=$81, mirror $0E6B); JML $80:8B66.
