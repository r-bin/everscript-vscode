Vendor dialogue check invoked in Nobilia Market (Room 10) prior to the Vigor colosseum battle.
Calls `$92:CC6C` to calculate the remaining minutes on the 15-minute countdown clock, copies the minutes value into `STRING_PARAMETER_2` (`$7E:2539`), and selects an increasingly urgent warning dialogue (plenty of time / moderate time / very little time / about to expire) to keep the player mindful of the approaching deadline.
