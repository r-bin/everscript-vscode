One word per expression sub-op `$00`-`$6F`: the routine (bank `$8C`) that reads its immediate bytes
and computes the new current value. `$19`, `$2F` and `$5D`-`$5F` point at `$8C:F03F`, a branch to
itself (the game would hang). The sub-ops: docs/game-research/script-vm/README.md.
