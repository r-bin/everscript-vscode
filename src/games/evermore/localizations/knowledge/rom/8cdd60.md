Expression: an entity address (>= $2000) gets the two words at +$68 / +$66; a small index n gets them at $0EA2 / $0EAC + 2(n-1) (the second word is skipped when the first is 0).

Operands: `E u16 u16` (tokens: docs/game-research/script-vm/README.md). Flow: continues in the same turn.

- Calls: `$8C:CE4D`

- Record fields (indexed by X/Y: a slot, an entity, a window): `+$66`, `+$68`
