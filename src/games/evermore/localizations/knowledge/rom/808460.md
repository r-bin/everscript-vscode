Part of the NMI handler's body. It adds one to the frame counter `$0100`, carrying into `$0102` when the
low word wraps. It writes the HDMA channel mask kept at direct page `$72` to `$420C`, so HDMA starts from
this frame's mask. Then it spins on `$4212` bit 0 until the automatic joypad read is done, so the joypad
registers the handler reads next are complete.

The spin makes the handler's length depend on when the hardware's joypad read ends, not only on the code.
An emulator or recomp that ends it at another clock leaves the handler early or late, and with it every
frame's idle phase.

## Notes
The direct page during the handler (for `$72`) was not checked. `$0102` as the counter's high word is read
from the code only.
