Piercing projectile hit resolution proc (index 4 in table `$8F:B6AE`).
Used by level 3 spear energy waves and special penetrating projectiles.

Evaluates to-hit via `$8F:B9F8`, calculates scaled damage via `$8F:C06D`, applies damage and reaction via `$8F:C0D3`, and knockback via `$8F:C17F`.
Unlike proc 2 (`$8F:B6E0`), it does NOT delete the projectile and returns Carry Set (`SEC`), allowing the projectile to continue through its trajectory and pierce multiple targets.
