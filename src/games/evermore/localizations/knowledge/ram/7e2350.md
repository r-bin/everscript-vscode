Companion separation and resting spot index in Antiqua.
When the Boy and Dog are split in Crustacia / pirate ship / pyramid quests, `DOG_SLEEP_SPOT` identifies which camp tile the Dog is sleeping at:
- `0`: Dog is with party / active
- `1`: Resting at spot 1 (tile `[11, 21]`)
- `2`: Resting at spot 2 (tile `[83, 21]`)
- `3`: Resting at spot 3 (tile `[87, 83]`)
- `4`: Resting at spot 4 (tile `[53, 79]`)

Cleared to 0 upon party reunification in the pyramid.
