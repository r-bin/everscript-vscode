Two 1-byte navigation coordinates tracking the player's logical grid position in the Desert of Doom (Room `0x1B`):
- `$7E:22FC`: Vertical Y sector index.
- `$7E:22FD`: Horizontal X sector index.

Room `0x1B` is a fixed 97x113 metatile room. The illusion of an infinite desert is achieved by four boundary trigger strips that increment/decrement these sector registers and reposition the player on the opposite boundary edge.
