Spinlock semaphore and handshake-in-progress counter for communication between the 65816 CPU and the SPC700 audio subsystem.

When sending commands (BGM change, volume ducking, sound effects) to hardware APU IO ports (`$2140`–`$2143`), `apu_handshake_2140_spinlock` increments `$0E51`, disables interrupts, polls the sync byte in `$7E:009F` against `$2142`, writes data words to `$2140` and `$2142`, decrements `$0E51`, and restores interrupts. Any non-zero value indicates that an audio transfer handshake is currently occupying the bus.
