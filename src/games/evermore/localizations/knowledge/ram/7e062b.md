Free for hacks as far as the recorded play goes (not a proof for unplayed parts). Inside
`$0000-$1FFF`, so it is also visible through the WRAM mirror of banks `$80-$BF`; that is what lets
a string key above `$3000` point into it. Other never-read runs in low WRAM: `$00E2-$00FF` (DP,
which everscript cannot write: `<0x00..0xff>` are its entity variables), `$0703-$07A3`,
`$04F1-$051C`.
