Persistent price surcharge counter for the rare relic vendor in Nobilia Market (Room 10, trigger 23 at [16,60]).

The vendor offers the Moxa Stick and Ruby Heart for a base cost of 25 (`0x19`) Talons plus the surcharge in `$7E:2369`:
`total_cost = 25 + <0x2369>`

After every successful transaction, the game adds 30 (`0x1E`) Talons to `$7E:2369`, capping out at 150 (`0x96`) Talons (resulting in a maximum purchase price of 175 Talons). Stored in persistent save RAM ($7E:2258..$7E:2833) so that inflation remains active across save files.
