Scratch RAM the Everscript compiler hands out to a room's script variables (`TEMP` in the linker, `$7E:2834`..`$7E:28FB`, 200 bytes).
It is cleared when a room loads, so nothing here survives a room change. At `$7E:28FC`, the 20 active thread slots of `struct ScriptSlot[20]` begin.
