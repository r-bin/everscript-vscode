Node structure (64 bytes) managing the animated floating damage and healing digit popups displayed over the Boy during combat.

Structure layout:
- `+0x00`: Screen X position
- `+0x02`: Screen Y position
- `+0x04`: Current vertical velocity / bounce phase
- `+0x06`: Lifetime duration ticks
- `+0x08`: 16-bit numerical damage or healing value
- `+0x0A`: Color attribute word (red for damage, green for healing, white for critical hits)
- `+0x0C`: Digit sprite tile base offset

Updated by `func_CF8A72` whenever the player takes damage or recovers HP.
