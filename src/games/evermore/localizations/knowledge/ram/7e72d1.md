WRAM staging buffer for dynamic 16x16 4bpp SNES sprite tiles queued for transfer to VRAM.

Structure:
- Stride: Exactly 128 bytes (64 words) per 16x16 tile (composed of four 8x8 4bpp tiles).
- Span: `$7E:72D1` to `$7E:7D50` (capacity for 21 dynamic 16x16 sprite tiles).
- Managed by `func_C096FE`. Staged tile blocks are streamed to SNES VRAM during VBlank or blanked display lines when large enemy, boss, or companion animations require new frames.
