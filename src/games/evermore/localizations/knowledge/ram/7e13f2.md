32-byte array of frame countdown timers for 32 active palette animation channels:
- Decremented every frame by `DEC $13F2,X` at `$90:D58E`.
- When non-zero, the channel skips updating for the current frame.
- When reaching zero, reloads from `$7E:13D2,X`, advances the color blend step counters, and streams the updated palette color words to CGRAM.
