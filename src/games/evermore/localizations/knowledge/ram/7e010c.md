8-bit shadow register for SNES PPU register `$210C` (`BG34NBA`):
- Controls the VRAM base word address for Background 3 and Background 4 character pattern tiles (used for the HUD overlay, dialogue boxes, and Mode 7 text layers).
- Maintained in WRAM at `$7E:010C` and updated synchronously during VBlank interrupt transfers.
