Palette `p` is at `$7E6187 + 32·p`, colour `c` at `+2c`, in CGRAM order. Background families take
`p = 1…7`, sprite palettes `p = 8…15`. Fades and flashes work on this copy, and CGRAM uploads read
it.

The exception is a newly loaded sprite palette: `$90:CFB8` uploads that one straight from ROM.
