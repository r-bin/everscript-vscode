Two words, x then y. A trigger box in the room data is stored in absolute 16-px cells; minus
this origin and times 16 it is in the same pixel space as the entity coordinates
(`$7E4EA3`/`$4EA5` for the Boy).

A step-on trigger only fires while the player walks onto it: writing the Boy's coordinates
straight into a trigger box did nothing until he took a step.
