512-byte audio subsystem work RAM and DSP interface staging buffer located at `$7E:2000`..`$7E:21FF`.
Stores per-channel volume attenuation settings, active sound command queues, stereo pan parameters, and music playback state updated during NMI audio sync dispatches.
