Controls gradual screen brightness fading. When bit 15 is set, the field engine's per-frame
update loop (`$80:DE95`) compares the low byte (target brightness, 0–15) with the current
`INIDISP` shadow at `$7E:0106`.

Each frame, the routine steps `$7E:0106` by +1 or -1 towards the target. Once the current
brightness matches the target, the routine zeroes `$7E:0B83` to stop the transition.

## Bits
| Bit | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | `FADE_ACTIVE` | Fade transition active (`0x8000`) |
| 0–3 | `TARGET_BRIGHTNESS` | Target PPU brightness level (0 = black, 15 = full brightness `$0F`) |

## Notes
Previously believed: in early reverse-engineering notes, this was hypothesized to be a
joypad control/action mask. Routine `$80:DE95` directly accesses `$7E:0106` (`$2100` `INIDISP` shadow)
and confirms it is exclusively for screen brightness transitions.
