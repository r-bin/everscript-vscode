Dirty flag asserted when the shadow palette buffer in WRAM (`$7E:013F`..`$7E:033E`) has been updated and requires a DMA copy to SNES CGRAM ($2121/$2122) during VBlank.

Inspected by `nmi_v_blank_handler` (`$C0:823A` / `$80:823A`). When set, the NMI handler resets the flag to zero and initiates a DMA transfer of modified palette data using channel 0/1.
