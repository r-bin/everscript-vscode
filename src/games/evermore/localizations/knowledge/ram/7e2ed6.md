35 contiguous 16-bit word registers storing the current level (0..9) of each alchemy formula.

Indexed by `formula_index * 2`:
- `+$00` (`$7E:2ED6`): Acid Rain
- `+$02` (`$7E:2ED8`): Atlas
- `+$04` (`$7E:2EDA`): Barrier
- …
- `+$44` (`$7E:2F1A`): Super Heal

Updated by `alchemy_award_cast_experience` (`$91:D850`) whenever accumulated formula XP reaches 100 points, capping at Level 9.
