Complete 512-byte (256 colors × 2 bytes) shadow buffer for SNES CGRAM color palettes in 15-bit BGR555 format.

Partitioning:
- `$7E:013F`..`$7E:023E`: Background palettes 0 through 7 (8 palettes × 16 colors × 2 bytes = 256 bytes)
- `$7E:023F`..`$7E:033E`: Sprite / OBJ palettes 0 through 7 (8 palettes × 16 colors × 2 bytes = 256 bytes)

During room loading or palette cycling animations, gameplay routines write color words into this WRAM mirror and assert `$7E:013B`. The VBlank NMI handler (`$C0:823A`) copies the mirror to CGRAM hardware registers.
