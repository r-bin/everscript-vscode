16-bit enumeration specifying the active atmospheric weather simulation system running in the current room:
- `0x0000`: Clear skies (weather particle loop bypassed).
- `0x0001`: Rain precipitation.
- `0x0002`: Falling snow.
- `0x0003`: Volcanic ash / burning embers.
- `0x0004`: Floating spores / desert dust.

When active, `func_80A305` integrates horizontal wind drift (`$7E:243B`) and downward velocity (`$7E:243D`) across 16 screen-space particles (`$7E:2440..$7E:247F`), wrapping around at scanline 224.
